Loop protection and memory you can see and clear.

* Repeating the exact same action is blocked with guidance back to the model; actions that change nothing on screen are stopped. A stuck run ends honestly.
* Older actions stay as a condensed journal, so long tasks never re-do work; the nudge no longer forces redoing a completed task.
* Settings shows every remembered fact and person: delete one by one or clear all. The agent can too, with forget and clear_memory.
