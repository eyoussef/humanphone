Auto mode hands the phone back:

* After finishing a task the agent used to leave the messaging app open in front — WhatsApp
  open in a chat posts no notifications for it, so the watchdog went deaf to the exact
  conversation it had just answered in.
* Now every finished run returns the phone home and brings HumanPhone back to the front:
  notifications flow again, automatically, whether the run ended with finish or used up its
  steps.
