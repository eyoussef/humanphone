The agent learns where your apps live, so daily tasks run faster.

* First time an app opens, the assistant remembers the name-to-package mapping. Next time it opens that app directly, without scanning every launcher or listing candidates.
* Every spelling the model uses is remembered, and re-use keeps it in memory (bounded, oldest one-offs drop). Uninstalled apps are re-learned automatically.
* Fewer probe steps: less waiting and fewer tokens per daily task.
