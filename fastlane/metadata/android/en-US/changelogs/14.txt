Auto mode fix:

* Notifications are heard again: a screen-watching update had silenced the notification
  stream, so Auto mode could not answer anything. It watches them once more.
* Messages that arrive while the agent is busy are queued and answered when the task ends,
  instead of being dropped.

Auto mode stays on watch:

* Turning Auto mode on starts the background monitor itself — no task or command needed.
* Closing the app no longer takes the monitor with it: the agent service keeps running and
  listening in the background, also after the last task was finalised.
* If the monitor went down, the next notification quietly brings it back.
